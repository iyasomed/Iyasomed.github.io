Foldable Electric Wheelchair YSE137 for Travel and Flights

Published on: 2026-08-13 | Author: Iyasocare

Traveling with a mobility aid should not mean giving up freedom, convenience, or independence. Whether you are planning a family vacation, taking a business trip, exploring a new city, or simply looking for a practical electric wheelchair for everyday use, portability can make all the difference.

The YSE137 Foldable Electric Wheelchair is designed around exactly this idea. Combining a lightweight folding structure, powerful dual brushless motors, a compact travel-friendly design, and a distinctive carbon-fiber-style finish, the YSE137 offers a practical mobility solution for users who value both functionality and convenience.

With a net weight of approximately 18.5 kg and a folding size of just 840 × 340 × 780 mm, the YSE137 is designed to make transportation and storage considerably easier than with many traditional powered wheelchairs.

A Foldable Electric Wheelchair Designed for Travel

One of the biggest advantages of an electric wheelchair is the freedom it can provide. However, a wheelchair that is difficult to transport can make traveling complicated.

The YSE137 addresses this challenge with its foldable design. When unfolded, the wheelchair measures approximately 840 × 540 × 950 mm. When folded, its dimensions are reduced to approximately 840 × 340 × 780 mm, making it much easier to place in a vehicle or store when not in use.

The folding structure is particularly useful for people who frequently travel between locations. Instead of needing a large dedicated space, the wheelchair can be folded into a more compact configuration for transportation and storage.

Its lightweight construction also contributes to its portability. The YSE137 has a net weight of approximately 18.5 kg, while the gross weight is around 20.5 kg, depending on packaging and configuration.

For travelers, this combination of foldability and relatively low weight can be a major advantage.

Designed with Air Travel in Mind

Air travel can be one of the biggest challenges for wheelchair users. Mobility equipment needs to be practical to transport, and travelers often look for a powered wheelchair that is specifically designed with portability in mind.

The YSE137 is designed to be carried on a plane, making it an attractive option for users who want to take their personal mobility equipment when traveling.

Its compact folding dimensions can make handling and transportation more convenient throughout a journey—from leaving home and loading the wheelchair into a vehicle to arriving at the airport and continuing the trip at the destination.

Of course, airline policies and battery requirements can vary between airlines and routes. Travelers should always confirm the specific airline's current requirements for powered wheelchairs and lithium batteries before flying.

Lightweight Construction with a Distinctive Carbon-Fiber Look

The YSE137 combines aluminum and carbon fiber in its frame construction, creating a balance between portability, structural performance, and appearance.

The frame features a hydrographics transfer printing carbon-fiber surface treatment. This gives the wheelchair a modern carbon-fiber-inspired appearance while helping it stand out from conventional mobility equipment.

The result is a wheelchair that doesn't simply focus on functionality—it also pays attention to style.

For users who want a mobility product that looks contemporary and sophisticated, the carbon-fiber-style finish provides a distinctive visual identity.

Compact Yet Comfortable Seating

A portable wheelchair should not sacrifice basic user comfort.

The YSE137 features a seat width of approximately 440 mm and a seat depth of approximately 410 mm. The back height is approximately 490 mm, providing support for the user's upper body.

The armrests are designed with a flip-up function, which adds another layer of convenience. Flip-up armrests can make getting into and out of the wheelchair easier and can also be useful when transferring between the wheelchair and another seat.

The cushion is available in black or yellow, allowing users or distributors to select a preferred appearance.

Two Powerful Brushless Motors

Portability is important, but an electric wheelchair also needs reliable drive performance.

The YSE137 is equipped with two 250W brushless motors, providing a combined motor output of 500W.

Brushless motors are well suited to electric mobility applications because they can provide efficient and smooth power delivery while avoiding the brushes used in traditional brushed motors.

With two motors driving the rear wheels, the YSE137 is designed to provide stable electric propulsion for everyday travel.

The wheelchair has a maximum speed of approximately 6 km/h, a practical speed for controlled mobility in suitable environments.

A Practical Driving Range for Everyday Travel

The YSE137 uses a 24V 6Ah lithium battery and provides a claimed driving range of approximately 15–20 km, depending on factors such as user weight, terrain, temperature, driving conditions, and battery condition.

For short trips, shopping excursions, indoor facilities, residential areas, and sightseeing, this range can provide useful flexibility.

The battery charging time is approximately 4–6 hours.

As with any battery-powered mobility device, users should plan their trips according to the actual conditions and recharge the battery before longer journeys.

Built for Different Types of Terrain

The YSE137 is equipped with 12-inch rear wheels and 8-inch front wheels.

The larger rear wheels contribute to stable movement, while the front wheels help the wheelchair maneuver over suitable surfaces.

The wheelchair has a stated climbing ability of up to 8°, making it capable of handling moderate inclines within its specified operating limits.

It also has an obstacle-crossing height of up to 40 mm.

These specifications make the YSE137 suitable for a variety of everyday environments, but users should always consider surface conditions, slopes, obstacles, weather, and the manufacturer's operating recommendations before attempting challenging terrain.

Automatic Electronic Braking for Added Convenience

Safety and control are essential characteristics of any powered wheelchair.

The YSE137 uses a fully automatic electronic brake system. The stated braking distance is ≤800 mm, under the applicable test conditions.

An automatic electronic braking system can provide convenient control when the user releases the driving control, helping the wheelchair remain stationary when power is no longer being applied.

As with all powered mobility equipment, users should become familiar with the controls and braking behavior in a safe, open area before using the wheelchair in busy environments.

A 90 kg Load Capacity

The YSE137 has a stated maximum load capacity of 90 kg.

This specification should be considered carefully when selecting the wheelchair. The total load should remain within the manufacturer's stated limit, taking into account the user and any items being carried.

Staying within the specified capacity helps ensure that the wheelchair operates according to its intended performance parameters.

A Practical Choice for Daily Mobility

Although the YSE137 is designed with travel in mind, its features can also make it useful for everyday mobility.

Imagine starting the morning with a trip to a local shopping center. The wheelchair can be unfolded from its compact configuration and prepared for use. After the trip, it can be folded again for transportation or storage.

For users who frequently move between a home, vehicle, hotel, shopping center, or other destinations, this flexibility can be extremely valuable.

Its compact folding dimensions also make it a practical option for people who have limited storage space.
