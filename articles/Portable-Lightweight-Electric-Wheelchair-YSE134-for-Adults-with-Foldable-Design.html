7. Electromagnetic Brake for Added Safety

Safety is one of the most important factors when purchasing an electric wheelchair.

This model is equipped with an electromagnetic braking system designed to automatically engage when the motor drive is released, helping prevent unintended movement when the wheelchair stops.

The electromagnetic brake provides convenient braking control without requiring the user to operate a conventional manual braking mechanism during normal powered driving.

Its braking system is designed to support a more stable and controlled mobility experience.

For safe operation, users should always follow the product manual, observe the recommended load capacity, and avoid steep slopes or uneven terrain beyond the wheelchair's specified capabilities.
