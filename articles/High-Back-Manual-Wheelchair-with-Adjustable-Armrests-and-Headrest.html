High Back Manual Wheelchair with Adjustable Armrests and Headrest

Published on: 2026-08-25 | Author: Iyasocare

For users who value stability, comfort, adjustability, and reliable everyday mobility, choosing the right manual wheelchair is about much more than simply selecting a seat with wheels. A well-designed wheelchair should provide appropriate body support, make transfers and positioning easier, and offer practical features for both the user and caregiver.

The High Back Manual Wheelchair with Adjustable Armrests and Headrest is designed around these needs. Combining a sturdy carbon-steel frame with PU leather upholstery, an adjustable high-back structure, detachable headrest, adjustable armrests, removable foot supports, and large rear wheels, this wheelchair offers a versatile solution for a wide range of daily mobility situations.

With a 46 cm seat width, 150 kg loading capacity, adjustable overall height of 121–133 cm, and 24-inch rear wheels, the model emphasizes both supportive seating and maneuverability.

A High-Back Design for Enhanced Upper-Body Support

One of the defining features of this wheelchair is its high-back configuration.

Compared with a standard low-back wheelchair, a high-back design provides additional support for the user's upper back, shoulders, and head. This can be especially useful when the wheelchair is being used for longer periods or when additional positioning support is desired.

The backrest angle can be adjusted, allowing the seating position to be adapted according to the user's needs. Rather than forcing every user into one fixed posture, the adjustable backrest gives caregivers greater flexibility when positioning the user.

The wheelchair also includes a detachable and adjustable headrest. Its height can be modified to provide more appropriate head and neck support, while the detachable design makes transportation, storage, and maintenance more convenient.

Adjustable Armrests for Greater Flexibility

Armrest height can have a significant effect on overall seating comfort.

If armrests are too high, the user's shoulders may be raised unnecessarily. If they are too low, the arms may lack adequate support. This wheelchair therefore incorporates height-adjustable armrests, making it possible to modify the arm position according to the user's body shape and seating requirements.

The adjustable armrests can also make the wheelchair more adaptable to different environments. For example, positioning the armrests appropriately can help users maintain a more natural seated posture while allowing caregivers to make adjustments when necessary.

The design focuses on practical flexibility rather than a one-size-fits-all configuration.

Adjustable Front and Rear Seating

Another notable feature is the adjustable front and rear seat configuration.

Seating position is an important part of wheelchair comfort. Small changes in seating geometry can influence posture, leg position, center of gravity, and overall user experience.

By providing adjustment options for the front and rear seating areas, this model gives caregivers greater flexibility when configuring the chair.

Together with the adjustable backrest and armrests, these features create a wheelchair that can be configured more precisely instead of relying entirely on fixed dimensions.

46 cm Seat Width and a 150 kg Loading Capacity

The wheelchair features a 46 cm seat width, providing a relatively spacious seating area while maintaining a practical overall product size.

Its specified loading capacity is 150 kg, making the frame suitable for users who require a higher load-bearing capacity.

When selecting a wheelchair, however, the stated maximum load should always be treated as a specification rather than a target. The user's weight, seating posture, accessories, and operating environment should all be considered when determining whether a particular model is appropriate.

Strong Carbon Steel Frame Construction

Durability starts with the frame.

This wheelchair uses a carbon steel frame and a sprayed frame finish. Carbon steel provides a strong structural foundation for a manual wheelchair and is well suited to a design intended to support substantial loads.

The frame is paired with PU leather upholstery, combining a practical surface material with the supportive structure of the steel frame.

PU leather can also make routine cleaning more convenient, which is an important consideration for a mobility product used regularly.

For long-term use, users should still follow the manufacturer's maintenance recommendations and regularly inspect the frame, fasteners, wheels, brakes, and other moving components.

24-Inch Rear Wheels for Manual Mobility

The wheelchair is equipped with 24-inch rear wheels, a traditional configuration for manual wheelchairs.

Larger rear wheels are useful because they provide the user or caregiver with a substantial wheel diameter for manual propulsion and control. The design also includes an inflatable rear wheel configuration with quick removal, which can make transportation and storage more convenient.

The ability to remove the rear wheels can be particularly useful when loading the wheelchair into a vehicle or when reducing its dimensions for storage.

The wheelchair's rear-wheel system is therefore designed not only for movement but also with practical handling and transportation in mind.

8-Inch Front Wheels

At the front, the wheelchair uses 8-inch front wheels with a TPR wheel design and plastic wheel frame.

The smaller front-wheel configuration complements the larger rear wheels, helping create a practical balance between maneuverability and stability.

Front casters play an important role in steering. Their size and construction affect how the wheelchair responds to changes in direction and different floor surfaces.

As with any manual wheelchair, users should take care when moving over uneven surfaces, thresholds, slopes, or other obstacles.

Rear Push Handles with Brakes

For caregiver-assisted mobility, the rear push handles are an important part of the overall design.

This model incorporates rear handles with brakes, allowing a caregiver to push and control the wheelchair while having access to braking functionality.

This can be especially useful when stopping the wheelchair, positioning it beside a bed or chair, or maintaining control while assisting the user.

The brakes should be engaged whenever the wheelchair is stationary and during transfers, according to the manufacturer's operating instructions. A wheelchair should never be left unattended on a slope simply because the brakes are engaged.

Removable Lifting Foot Supports

Lower-limb positioning is another important consideration.

The wheelchair features removable lifting foot supports, giving caregivers additional flexibility when assisting the user.

A removable foot-support system can make transfers and access easier in certain situations. It can also provide greater flexibility when the wheelchair needs to be positioned close to furniture or when the user's legs require a particular arrangement.

The lifting function is designed to support practical positioning and improve overall usability.

A Practical Design for Caregivers

A wheelchair is not used only by the person sitting in it. In many situations, caregivers, family members, nurses, and other assistants are also responsible for moving, positioning, storing, and maintaining the chair.

This model includes several features aimed at making those tasks more manageable:

  • Adjustable and detachable headrest
  • Adjustable armrest height
  • Adjustable backrest angle
  • Adjustable front and rear seating
  • Removable lifting foot supports
  • Quick-removable rear wheels
  • Rear push handles
  • Rear brakes

These features give caregivers more options for adapting the wheelchair to different users and environments.

Important Considerations Before Purchase

Although specifications can tell you a great deal about a wheelchair, choosing the correct model should always involve more than comparing numbers.

1. Seat width

The 46 cm seat width should be compared with the user's body dimensions and preferred seating position. The user should have sufficient room without excessive lateral space.

2. Seat height

The 58 cm seat height should be considered in relation to the user's leg length, transfer ability, and the height of frequently used furniture.

3. Overall height

Because the wheelchair's total height is adjustable between 121 and 133 cm, users should consider the desired headrest and back-support configuration as well as storage conditions.

4. Wheelchair weight

At 38.1 kg net, the chair is designed more toward sturdy functionality than lightweight portability. Anyone who regularly needs to lift the wheelchair should take this into account.

5. Loading capacity

The specified maximum loading capacity is 150 kg. The total load should remain within the manufacturer's stated limit, and safe operation should always follow the manufacturer's instructions.

6. Environment

Consider where the wheelchair will primarily be used. Indoor flooring, outdoor pavement, thresholds, ramps, narrow doorways, elevators, and vehicle access can all influence whether the dimensions and wheel configuration are suitable.

Maintenance for Long-Term Performance

Regular maintenance is essential for any manual wheelchair.

The wheels should be inspected periodically, especially the inflatable rear wheels. Check tire condition and inflation according to the manufacturer's recommendations.

The brakes should also be checked regularly to make sure they engage properly.

Moving components such as the adjustable armrests, foot supports, headrest, backrest mechanisms, and quick-release wheels should be inspected for looseness or damage.

The PU leather upholstery should be kept clean and dry, while the carbon-steel frame should be checked for signs of damage, corrosion, or structural problems.

If any component appears damaged or does not operate correctly, the wheelchair should not be used until it has been appropriately inspected or repaired.
