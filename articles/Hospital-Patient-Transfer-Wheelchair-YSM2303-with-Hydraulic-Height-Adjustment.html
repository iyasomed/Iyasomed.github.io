The hydraulic adjustment function allows caregivers to raise or lower the seat to a more suitable position.

1. More flexible patient transfers

Caregivers can adjust the seat height to better match the height of a bed or another transfer surface, helping facilitate patient movement.

2. Improved caregiver ergonomics

A suitable transfer height may reduce the need for excessive bending and awkward working positions during certain care procedures.

3. Adaptability to different environments

From hospital wards to nursing homes and home care settings, adjustable height makes the wheelchair more versatile for different care requirements.

4. Convenient foot-operated operation

The hydraulic foot pedal allows caregivers to operate the lifting mechanism using their feet, keeping their hands available for patient support.

With a height adjustment range of 23 cm, the YSM2303 provides greater flexibility than a conventional fixed-height patient chair.

12. Important Considerations Before Using a Hydraulic Patient Transfer Wheelchair

Although multifunctional transfer chairs can help simplify daily care, correct operation is essential.

Before using the YSM2303, caregivers should consider the following points:

  1. Check the maximum load: Ensure that the patient's weight does not exceed the stated 100 kg capacity.
  2. Inspect the wheels and brakes: Confirm that the wheels move properly and that the braking system functions correctly.
  3. Adjust the seat height carefully: Use the hydraulic pedal according to the manufacturer's operating instructions.
  4. Check the transfer position: Ensure the chair is appropriately positioned and stable before assisting the patient.
  5. Follow safe transfer procedures: Patient transfers should be performed by trained caregivers using suitable techniques.
  6. Maintain hygiene: Clean and disinfect the commode components and other relevant surfaces according to the manufacturer's recommendations.
  7. Perform regular maintenance: Inspect the hydraulic cylinder, frame, fasteners, wheels, and braking components periodically.

The wheelchair should only be used for its intended purpose and in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions. It should not be assumed to replace specialized patient lifting equipment when a full-body lift is required.
