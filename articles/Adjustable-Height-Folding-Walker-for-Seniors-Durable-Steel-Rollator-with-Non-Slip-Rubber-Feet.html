Adjustable Height for Different Users

One of the most useful features of the YSW7002 is its adjustable height system.

The walker provides 8 height adjustment holes, allowing the legs to be positioned at different heights. The supplied specifications indicate an adjustable range of approximately 18 cm, while the product description also references an 8 cm adjustment range depending on production specifications.

How to Adjust the Height

Before using the walker, make sure all four legs are adjusted to the same height.

Press the adjustment buttons or spring-loaded mechanisms on the legs, select the desired position, and make sure the locking mechanism has fully engaged.

It is important to check all four legs before putting weight on the walker. Uneven adjustment may affect stability and should be avoided.

Simple Folding and Opening Instructions

Using the folding mechanism correctly is essential for safe operation.

Step 1: Fold or Open the Walker

When opening the walker, unfold the frame completely.

The middle locking mechanism should move into position, with the locking pin or spring-loaded mechanism engaging securely.

Do not use the walker until you have confirmed that the frame is fully opened and locked.

Step 2: Adjust All Four Legs

Press the adjustment mechanisms on the four legs one at a time.

Select the desired height and make sure each leg is adjusted to the same position.

Check that all adjustment pins are fully engaged before use.

Step 3: Check the Walker

Before walking, inspect the frame, handles, wheels, rubber feet, adjustment holes, and locking mechanisms.

Make sure there are no loose components or damaged parts.

Step 4: Hold the Handles Securely

Place both hands firmly on the handles.

Maintain a comfortable upright posture and keep the walker at a suitable distance in front of your body.

Step 5: Move Forward Gradually

The supplied instructions recommend moving forward alternately with the left and right hands.

Move slowly and maintain control of the walker.

Users who are unfamiliar with a wheeled walker may benefit from professional guidance from a qualified healthcare or mobility professional.

Important Safety Considerations

A walker is a mobility support device, so proper operation and regular maintenance are important.

Before every use, check that:

  • The frame is fully opened and locked.
  • All four legs are adjusted to the same height.
  • The adjustment pins are securely engaged.
  • The handles are firmly attached.
  • The wheels move properly.
  • The rubber feet are intact and not excessively worn.
  • There are no cracks, bends, loose screws, or damaged components.
  • The user remains within the stated maximum load capacity.
  • The walking surface is reasonably stable and free from major obstacles.

Never use a damaged walker.

Users should also avoid sudden movements, excessive leaning, or placing more weight on the walker than it is designed to support.

Why Adjustability Matters

No two users are exactly the same.

A walker that is too low may encourage the user to bend forward unnecessarily. A walker that is too high may make the handles uncomfortable to reach and can reduce control.

That is why adjustable legs are such an important feature.

With multiple height positions, the YSW7002 offers flexibility for different users and different physical requirements.

The correct height should provide a comfortable hand position while allowing the user to maintain an appropriate posture.

When there is uncertainty about the correct height, professional assessment can be helpful.

Maintenance Tips for Longer Service Life

Proper maintenance can help preserve the condition and performance of a walker.

Keep the Frame Clean

Wipe the frame regularly with a suitable soft cloth. Avoid using harsh chemicals that could damage the surface or components.

Inspect the Rubber Feet

Rubber foot pads are subject to normal wear. Check them regularly for cracks, excessive wear, deformation, or looseness.

Check the Adjustment Mechanisms

Make sure the spring buttons or adjustment pins operate correctly and engage completely.

Inspect the Wheels

Check that the wheels rotate properly and that there is no debris interfering with their movement.

Check the Folding Lock

Before each use, make sure the folding mechanism is securely locked in the open position.

Store Properly

When not in use, store the walker in a clean, dry location and avoid exposing it unnecessarily to extreme environmental conditions.
