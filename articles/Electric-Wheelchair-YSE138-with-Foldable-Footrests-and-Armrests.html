Electric Wheelchair YSE138 with Foldable Footrests and Armrests

Published on: 2026-08-18 | Author: Iyasocare

Mobility should never be limited by distance, uneven ground, or the challenge of transporting a traditional wheelchair. For users who need a practical powered mobility solution that combines portability, comfort, stability, and easy operation, the YSE138 Electric Wheelchair offers a thoughtfully designed option for everyday travel.

Featuring a lightweight aluminum alloy frame, dual brushless motors, a lithium battery, foldable armrests and footrests, and a compact folding structure, the YSE138 is designed to make powered mobility more convenient both indoors and outdoors.

Whether it is used at home, in shopping centers, on sidewalks, during family outings, or while traveling, this compact electric wheelchair is built around one important idea: making independent mobility easier.

A Compact Electric Wheelchair Designed for Everyday Use

Traditional powered wheelchairs can be difficult to transport because of their weight and bulky construction. The YSE138 takes a different approach by combining an aluminum alloy frame with a folding structure.

The wheelchair measures approximately 930 × 570 × 930 mm in its normal configuration. When folded, its dimensions are reduced to approximately 570 × 370 × 760 mm, making it much easier to store and transport.

At approximately 20 kg net weight and 23.5 kg gross weight, the YSE138 is designed with portability in mind. Its folding configuration can be particularly useful for users and families who need to place a wheelchair in a vehicle, store it at home, or move it between different locations.

The compact folding design also makes the wheelchair suitable for environments where storage space is limited.

Lightweight Aluminum Alloy Frame

One of the key features of the YSE138 is its aluminum alloy frame.

Aluminum alloy is widely used in mobility equipment because it offers a useful balance between strength and weight. For an electric wheelchair, reducing unnecessary weight can make a significant difference when the product needs to be lifted, transported, or stored.

The YSE138 uses a sand black aluminum alloy frame, giving the wheelchair a modern and understated appearance while maintaining a practical structure.

With a maximum load capacity of 100 kg, the frame is designed for everyday mobility applications while keeping the overall wheelchair relatively lightweight.

Foldable Design for Easier Transportation

Portability is one of the biggest advantages of the YSE138.

The wheelchair features a folding structure that reduces its overall footprint from approximately 930 × 570 × 930 mm to around 570 × 370 × 760 mm.

This can be especially useful when:

  • Traveling by car
  • Storing the wheelchair at home
  • Taking the wheelchair on family trips
  • Moving between different rooms or buildings
  • Transporting the wheelchair when not in use
  • Saving storage space in apartments or smaller homes

A folding electric wheelchair can offer an important advantage over larger fixed-frame power wheelchairs because transportation does not necessarily require a large dedicated space.

Foldable Footrests and Adjustable Leg Design

The YSE138 is equipped with foldable footrests, helping reduce the wheelchair's size during storage and transportation.

The foldable leg design also contributes to easier access and handling. For users and caregivers, a wheelchair that can be configured more conveniently can make everyday transfers and storage less complicated.

The leg height is designed to be foldable, adding another practical feature to the overall mobility system.

Flip-Up Armrests for Greater Convenience

Another useful feature is the rear flip armrest design.

The armrests feature PU armrest pads, providing a soft and practical surface for supporting the user's arms during normal operation.

Flip-up armrests can also provide greater flexibility when approaching the wheelchair from the side or when transferring between a wheelchair and another seat.

Combined with the folding footrests, this design gives the YSE138 a more flexible configuration for everyday use.

Comfortable Seating for Everyday Mobility

Comfort is an important consideration for any wheelchair, particularly when it is used for extended periods.

The YSE138 has a seat width of approximately 380 mm, a seat depth of approximately 430 mm, and a seat height of approximately 510 mm.

The back height is approximately 410 mm, providing support for the user's back while maintaining the compact proportions of the wheelchair.

The wheelchair comes with a black cushion, creating a simple, neutral appearance that works well with the sand black frame.

For users who spend significant amounts of time in a wheelchair, seating comfort should always be considered alongside factors such as posture, fit, and individual support requirements.

Dual 200W Brushless Motors

Powering the YSE138 are two 200W brushless motors.

The dual-motor configuration provides independent power to the rear wheels and is designed to deliver smooth powered movement.

Brushless motors are a popular choice for electric mobility products because they are designed for efficient operation while reducing some of the maintenance requirements associated with traditional brushed motors.

With two motors working together, the YSE138 is designed to provide reliable propulsion for everyday mobility.

The maximum speed is approximately 6 km/h, making it suitable for controlled mobility in appropriate environments.

24V 10Ah Lithium Battery

The YSE138 uses a 24V 10Ah lithium battery.

Lithium battery technology allows the wheelchair to maintain a relatively compact and lightweight power system while providing the energy needed for daily electric mobility.

The specified charging time is approximately 4–6 hours.

Depending on factors such as user weight, terrain, temperature, driving habits, battery condition, and operating conditions, the stated driving range is approximately 10–20 km.

This range can make the YSE138 suitable for a variety of everyday trips, although actual performance will naturally vary according to real-world conditions.

Up to 20 km of Driving Range

For a compact folding electric wheelchair, range is an important consideration.

The YSE138 is specified with a driving range of approximately 10–20 km on a full charge.

This can provide flexibility for everyday activities such as:

  • Shopping trips
  • Visits to parks
  • Community activities
  • Indoor and outdoor mobility
  • Short-distance travel
  • Family outings
  • Daily errands

Front Shock Absorption for Improved Riding Comfort

Outdoor surfaces are not always perfectly smooth. Small cracks, pavement transitions, and other surface irregularities can affect riding comfort.

The YSE138 incorporates a front wheel shock absorber system to help manage impacts from uneven surfaces.

The front wheels measure approximately 7 inches, while the rear wheels measure approximately 12 inches.

The combination of front suspension and larger rear wheels is designed to provide a more comfortable and controlled ride across suitable everyday surfaces.

9° Climbing Ability

The YSE138 has a specified maximum climbing ability of approximately 9°.

This allows the wheelchair to handle certain inclines encountered during everyday mobility.

However, users should always approach ramps and slopes carefully. The actual ability to climb an incline can be affected by the user's weight, battery charge, surface condition, traction, weather, and the condition of the wheelchair.

For safety, users should avoid slopes or terrain that exceed the wheelchair's recommended operating capabilities.

Electronic Braking System

Safety is an essential part of electric wheelchair design.

The YSE138 uses an electronic brake system designed to help control the wheelchair when the user releases the controls.

The specified braking distance is ≤800 mm, under the applicable test conditions for the product specification.

Electronic braking can provide convenient and responsive control during everyday driving. Nevertheless, users should maintain an appropriate speed and leave sufficient stopping distance, particularly on slopes, wet surfaces, or crowded pathways.

Obstacle-Crossing Capability

The YSE138 has a specified obstacle-crossing height of up to 40 mm.

This feature is useful when dealing with small changes in surface height, such as certain pavement transitions or thresholds.

However, obstacle-crossing ability depends on the specific obstacle and driving conditions. Users should approach obstacles slowly and directly rather than attempting to cross large or irregular objects at speed.

Rear PU Wheels

The YSE138 is equipped with rear PU wheels.

Polyurethane wheels are often selected for mobility products because they can provide a practical balance between durability, rolling performance, and maintenance convenience.

The rear wheels measure 12 inches, giving the wheelchair a stable foundation for powered movement.

Combined with the 7-inch front wheels and front shock absorber, the wheel configuration is designed for practical everyday mobility.
