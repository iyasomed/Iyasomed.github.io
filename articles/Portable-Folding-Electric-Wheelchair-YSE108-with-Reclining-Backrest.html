Portable Folding Electric Wheelchair YSE108 with Reclining Backrest

Published on: 2026-09-24 | Author: Iyasocare

Mobility plays an important role in maintaining independence, comfort, and quality of life. For people who have difficulty walking long distances or require assistance with daily transportation, a reliable electric wheelchair can make everyday activities much easier.

The Portable Folding Electric Wheelchair YSE108 with Reclining Backrest is designed with mobility, comfort, convenience, and practical functionality in mind. Combining a sturdy carbon steel frame, dual 250W motors, pneumatic tires, a reclining backrest, adjustable footrest, flip-up armrests, and a folding design, the YSE108 is built to provide users with a versatile mobility solution for both indoor and outdoor use.

With a maximum load capacity of 130 kg, a travel range of approximately 15 km, and a folding structure that makes transportation and storage easier, this electric wheelchair offers a combination of useful features for users who need powered mobility without giving up flexibility.

1. A Folding Electric Wheelchair Designed for Everyday Mobility

One of the most practical features of the YSE108 is its folding design.

Traditional electric wheelchairs can be difficult to transport because of their size and weight. The YSE108 addresses this challenge with a foldable frame that can be reduced to approximately 82 × 35 × 75 cm, depending on the configuration.

This compact folded size can make the wheelchair easier to store when it is not being used. It can also be useful when preparing for trips, family outings, shopping, or other activities where transportation and storage space are important considerations.

The folding mechanism is designed to make the wheelchair easier to handle while maintaining the structural stability needed during use.

For households with limited storage space, a foldable electric wheelchair can be particularly convenient because it can occupy significantly less space when stored.

2. Strong Carbon Steel Frame and 130 kg Load Capacity

The YSE108 uses a carbon steel frame, providing a solid structural foundation for the wheelchair.

The stated maximum load capacity is 130 kg, making the chair suitable for a broad range of adult users, provided the user's weight and body dimensions are within the manufacturer's recommended specifications.

The frame combines strength with a practical design intended for everyday mobility.

The overall product dimensions are approximately:

  • Product size: 113 × 63 × 126 cm
  • Seat width: 46 cm
  • Seat depth: approximately 43–45 cm
  • Seat height: approximately 54–56 cm
  • Backrest height: approximately 76–80 cm
  • Folded size: approximately 82 × 35 × 75 cm
  • Maximum load: 130 kg

These dimensions provide a relatively spacious seating area while keeping the wheelchair compact enough to fold for transportation and storage.

Because the specifications list slightly different seat and backrest dimensions in different sections, buyers should confirm the final production configuration with the supplier before ordering.

3. Dual 250W Motors for Powered Mobility

The YSE108 is equipped with two 250W electric motors, providing a combined rated motor power of 500W.

The dual-motor configuration allows the wheelchair to drive using independent powered wheels while providing the traction required for normal mobility.

An important feature is the electromagnetic braking system.

Electromagnetic brakes are designed to provide controlled stopping when the motor power is released. This can be particularly useful for everyday operation, helping the wheelchair remain stable when the user stops moving.

The combination of dual motors and electromagnetic brakes provides a practical balance between propulsion and control.

However, users should always operate the wheelchair according to the manufacturer's instructions and avoid terrain or slopes beyond the product's specified operating limits.

4. 15 km Travel Range for Daily Activities

The YSE108 is specified with a travel range of approximately 15 km.

Actual range can vary depending on a number of factors, including:

  • User weight
  • Terrain
  • Inclines and declines
  • Driving speed
  • Tire pressure
  • Battery condition
  • Temperature
  • Frequency of stopping and starting
  • Surface conditions

For everyday activities such as moving around a home, visiting nearby locations, shopping areas, parks, or other accessible environments, the stated range can provide useful mobility.

The wheelchair uses a 12Ah lead-acid battery, with the battery weight listed at approximately 9 kg.

Lead-acid batteries are a traditional battery technology commonly used in mobility equipment because of their established availability and relatively straightforward charging requirements.

For best battery performance and service life, users should follow the manufacturer's charging and maintenance instructions.

5. Reclining Backrest for Improved Seating Comfort

Comfort is one of the key characteristics of the YSE108.

The wheelchair features an electrically controlled adjustment system for the backrest and footrest, with the backrest capable of reclining to approximately 160 degrees.

This reclining function allows users to adjust their seating position according to their needs.

Instead of remaining in a single upright position, the user can change the backrest angle to create a more relaxed posture.

This can be especially useful during longer periods of sitting.

The high backrest provides additional upper-body support, while the reclining function allows users to customize the seating position.

High Backrest That Can Be Converted to a Lower Configuration

Another practical feature is that the high backrest can be removed or adjusted into a lower-backrest configuration.

This gives the wheelchair additional flexibility depending on the user's preferred seating position and usage scenario.

A higher backrest can provide more support, while a lower configuration may offer a more open seating experience.

This adjustable design makes the YSE108 more adaptable to different user preferences.

6. Electrical Backrest and Footrest Adjustment

The YSE108 goes beyond basic forward and backward movement by incorporating electrical control for the backrest and footrest adjustment.

This means users can change their seating position without manually repositioning the backrest.

The electrically controlled adjustment system can be particularly convenient for users who have limited upper-body strength or difficulty reaching and manually adjusting wheelchair components.

Together, the reclining backrest and adjustable footrest create a more customizable seating environment.

Users can adjust the chair according to their activity, whether they are sitting upright for mobility or reclining for relaxation.

7. Flip-Up Armrests for Easier Transfers

The wheelchair is equipped with flip-up armrests.

This seemingly simple feature can make a significant difference in daily use.

When the armrests are raised, the sides of the seating area become more accessible, which can make transfers between the wheelchair and a bed, chair, sofa, or other surface more convenient.

The flip-up design can also make it easier for caregivers to assist the user.

In addition, raising the armrests can provide greater flexibility when positioning the wheelchair next to a table or desk.

8. 16-Inch Rear Pneumatic Tires

The YSE108 features 16-inch rear pneumatic tires mounted on aluminum rims.

Large rear wheels can contribute to smoother movement over many common outdoor surfaces.

The pneumatic tire design provides a degree of cushioning because air inside the tire helps absorb vibration from uneven surfaces.

This works together with the wheelchair's independent shock absorption system to improve overall ride comfort.

The rear wheels use aluminum rims, helping provide a combination of structural strength and practical wheel construction.

The 16-inch rear wheels also give the wheelchair a substantial wheel diameter compared with many compact mobility products.

9. 10-Inch Aluminum-Alloy Front Wheels

At the front, the wheelchair uses 10-inch aluminum-alloy wheels.

The larger-than-small-caster design helps provide stability while moving across ordinary indoor and outdoor surfaces.

The aluminum-alloy construction offers a durable wheel structure while keeping the front wheel assembly practical for everyday operation.

Together, the 10-inch front wheels and 16-inch pneumatic rear wheels create a balanced wheel configuration designed for comfortable movement.

10. Independent Shock Absorption for a More Comfortable Ride

Uneven surfaces can be one of the biggest challenges when using a mobility device outdoors.

The YSE108 addresses this with an independent shock absorption system.

Shock absorption helps reduce the impact transmitted to the wheelchair and user when traveling across surfaces that are not perfectly smooth.

Combined with pneumatic tires, the suspension system is intended to provide a more comfortable ride.

Whether traveling over pavement, paths, thresholds, or other common surfaces, the suspension and tire combination can help reduce vibration and improve overall driving comfort.

Of course, the wheelchair should still be used only on surfaces and slopes appropriate for its specifications.

11. Manual Mode for Added Flexibility

Another useful feature of the YSE108 is the ability to switch to manual mode.

This gives the wheelchair additional flexibility when the electric drive system is not being used.

Manual mode can be useful in situations where the chair needs to be moved without relying on the electric motors.

For example, a caregiver may need to reposition the wheelchair indoors or move it when the battery is unavailable.

Having both electric and manual operating modes makes the YSE108 more versatile for everyday situations.

12. Designed With Practical Transportation in Mind

The combination of folding functionality and manual mode makes the YSE108 a practical option for users who frequently need to move or store their wheelchair.

The listed net weight is approximately 46 kg without the battery, while the gross weight with the 12Ah lead-acid battery is approximately 58 kg.

It is important to note that this is still a substantial weight, so users should plan transportation appropriately. Assistance may be required when lifting or loading the wheelchair.

The folding structure can nevertheless make the product easier to fit into suitable storage or transportation spaces compared with a full-size, non-folding electric wheelchair.

13. A High Backrest for Additional Support

The YSE108 is designed with a relatively high backrest, listed at approximately 76–80 cm, depending on the configuration.

A high backrest can provide additional support for the upper body and back.

For users who spend extended periods sitting, proper seating support is particularly important.

The combination of the high backrest, reclining mechanism, adjustable footrest, and armrests allows the seating position to be customized to the user's preferences.

The ability to change the backrest configuration further expands the wheelchair's versatility.

14. A Practical Seating Area

The seat width is approximately 46 cm, while the seat depth is listed as approximately 43–45 cm.

This provides a reasonably spacious seating area while maintaining a compact overall structure.

The appropriate seat size is important because a wheelchair should provide sufficient support without being excessively wide or deep for the user.

Before purchasing, users should compare their own body dimensions with the manufacturer's final seat measurements.
